# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Blaster listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as
the plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-blaster](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-blaster).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `blaster-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome,
then downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Slides

| # | Slide | Shows |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Cover — name, tagline, app icon, free badge | — |
| 2 | `if ($visitor->…)` → `data-blaster-config` | the whole pitch, in one line |
| 3 | Seven targeting rules, labelled server or browser | where each condition is decided |
| 4 | Four candidate bars, two positions, two winners | priority, and one bar per position |
| 5 | Version 4 dismissed → version 5 shown again | why editing a bar brings it back |
| 6 | Twenty-seven capabilities, none behind a licence | one edition, everything on |
| 7 | `bars/list` and `stats/prune` in a terminal | three integers per bar, per day |

## Notes

Blaster is **free with no editions**, so like Bed and unlike the paid plugins in the family there
is no price on the cover badge and no figure anywhere in the deck that a pricing change could
strand.

The **watermark is an outline, and the icon's tile is stripped** — two separate corrections, both
needed. The tile goes because at slide scale its edge is a hard rectangle laid over the artwork.
The fill goes because a filled ray gun at 5% opacity is a soft grey mass: tried it, and the barrel
ridges and the trigger guard that make the shape legible all vanish into it. Stroked, the same
paths are line art and every segment reads.

The watermark also had to be **resized and moved** when the icon changed. The megaphone it replaced
sat at 1180px from top -12% / left -13%; the gun is a wide, short shape and that crop cut the grip
off the left edge, leaving barrel ridges floating — legible as *something*, not as a ray gun.
900px from top 2% / left -4% puts the whole mark on the slide.

Three layout rules the deck was retuned for after the first render:

- **One line per bullet, about 58 characters at 25px.** The copy column is 780px inside a 130px
  gutter and the panel starts at `left: 900px`, so a bullet that wraps both runs at the panel and
  pushes the last point towards the footer lockup — and nothing clips, the text simply lands on
  top. Five of the twenty-one bullets had to be cut back.
- **`.points li` is a flex container.** An inline `<span class="mono">` or `<em>` left loose in
  the text becomes a *sibling flex item* and picks up the 16px gap on both sides. Every point
  keeps its text in one `<span class="t">`.
- **`.swap .after` is 54px, not Bed's 74px.** Blaster's after-line is a whole attribute plus a
  comment rather than one short declaration, and at 74px it ran the full 1920 and touched both
  edges.

**A dashed 1px border does not read at 1920.** Slide 3 marks the browser-side rules with one, and
at slide scale they are indistinguishable from the plain rows — so the copy points at the
`SERVER` / `BROWSER` labels and the filled backgrounds, which do read, rather than at the dashes.
On slide 6 the same distinction survives only because those chips are also *coloured*.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh`, which base64-inlines the two woff2 files in `assets/` —
Chrome's `file://` origin rules block them otherwise. It is gitignored; don't edit it.
